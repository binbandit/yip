# Conversation and catch-up campaign, 28 September 2026

This pass tested whether ordinary conversation still leads the product: the
owner can return, understand what changed, ask a follow-up and inspect a result
without becoming the team's dispatcher. The comparison used the owner's
[original shared conversation](https://chatgpt.com/s/cx_6aba1b54dcb881919ae6ec6475462aa9),
successfully opened in Chrome, alongside the
[product specification](../spec/02-product-and-design.md) and its
[research and evidence](../spec/04-research-and-evidence.md).

The work used disposable hubs and browser fixtures. Scripted demo scenes,
mounted-app regressions and a real-provider recall are identified separately.
The earlier [adversarial campaign](2026-09-28.md) retains the broader GitHub,
permissions, recovery and daily-workspace evidence; this report does not
recount those results as new UX coverage.

These changes use the existing conversation, work and evidence surfaces.
They add no human inbox or mandatory handoff ritual. The Workspace summary
is deliberately narrower than general question answering: it is a factual
snapshot, not an autonomous manager or a substitute for talking with an
engineer.

At the owner's request, the persistent demo banner and its notice wrapper
were removed from the main frame. Connection failures and retry status
remain visible. Setup now gives the actual provider limitation, including
account, billing or read-only capability, while preserving completed setup
milestones when the machine is unavailable.

## Browser and regression evidence

One Chrome tab stayed on Overview while another submitted the Atlas expiry
assignment. The untouched Overview showed the new active job, then its
completed result and accepted decision without navigation or reload. The
visit time remained fixed. The fast intermediate review stage is covered by
the mounted-app regression, rather than claimed from a screenshot taken
after it finished.

An actual owner-question journey followed the answer back into the original
conversation, resolved the question and resumed the dependent job. Overview's
Needs a look count fell from two to one, retaining the unrelated failure.
The reviewer-question regression separately verifies that the question stays
attached to its parent assignment while it is open.

Chrome room checks covered 390, 900, 1280 and 1440px in both day and night
themes, with no horizontal overflow. On the tablet layout, Escape closed the
evidence drawer and restored focus to the work row that opened it. The
follow-up journey also kept a finished assignment's draft separate from a
second, unrelated assignment owned by the same engineer.

## Real-provider recall

A separate read-only Claude conversation used the saved real-team workspace
from the earlier campaign. The prompt intentionally misnamed the second
feature as a “batch-session helper.” The answer corrected that assumption,
identified the actual `remaining_seconds(now, expires)` helper, and recalled
both reviewed revisions: expiry fix `d098f10e` and helper `46cd64c6`. It
correctly said both remained local, with nothing pushed, merged or deployed,
and retained the optional reviewer suggestion about a fractional after-expiry
test.

The answer could not establish which review happened last and said so instead
of choosing a revision. It also exceeded the requested 120-word limit. These
are bounded model limitations alongside the correct revisions and publication
state. The run completed with no active runs remaining.
The [machine-readable result](2026-09-28-ux-real-recall.json) preserves the
request, answer and full revision IDs. This is evidence for one saved-history
conversation, not a general benchmark of memory, chronology or every provider.

## Product assessment and limits

The observed return-to-work loop now agrees across the conversation,
Overview and evidence drawers. A selected draft keeps its engineer and work
context, the team can complete routine review without asking the owner to
coordinate it, and genuine questions link back to their source. These are
concrete improvements toward the original conversation-led product.

The tests do not establish that every casually phrased request will infer the
right intent. Room reply mode, structured recipients and selected work still
matter; an ordinary message in a mentions-only room can remain ordinary chat.
The explicit Workspace summary provides known status. General questions and
explanations still require an engineer conversation.

The real recall exposed a rendering defect with bold text containing inline
code. The exact-answer regression passes after the parser fix; captures made
before that fix retain the delimiters and are labelled in the screenshot index.

This pass does not establish enterprise SSO, multiple human roles, arbitrary
workflow coverage or unattended operation on several physical machines.
The product still requires deliberate room membership, project access and
provider configuration. The guide makes those choices coherent; it does not
remove their necessity. Model judgment and provider capability limits from
the earlier campaign still apply.

## Reproduction entry points

- `web/tests/unit/overview.live.test.ts`: one persistent visit, live review and
  completion, accepted decisions, question classification and threaded sources.
- `web/tests/unit/approval.stale.test.ts`: another client decides a permission;
  the visible card reconciles approved, delivered, changed or unknown state.
- `web/tests/unit/composer-lifecycle.test.ts`: selected work ends while a draft
  exists, survives reload, and remains targeted after a failed send.
- `web/tests/unit/setup.test.ts`: connected setup, review access, live provider
  availability and restoration of a hidden guide.
- `web/tests/unit/overview-summary.test.ts`: failed summary recovery in the
  Overview aside and its room view.
- `web/tests/unit/overview-review-question.test.ts`: a reviewer's open question
  appears under the original assignment with its exact conversation link.
- `test/integration/overview_ux_test.go`: preserved catch-up baseline and the
  explicit summary API contract.
- `test/integration/overview_questions_test.go`: genuine questions raised
  during review remain visible and cease blocking after they are answered.
- `test/integration/workspace_sessions_test.go`: independent sessions for
  separate workspace identities.
